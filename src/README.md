# Carta — Play Smart. Compete Live. Win Together.

Carta is a fast, social card game built for Telegram. Create a room, invite friends, challenge real players, practice against an adaptive bot, and enjoy competitive rounds with clear Birr entry fees and payouts.

## Why players choose Carta

- **Live multiplayer:** Play exciting two-, three-, or four-player games.
- **Public and private rooms:** Join the community or invite only the people you choose.
- **Adaptive bot challenge:** The bot becomes more competitive as your experience and wins grow.
- **Fast rematches:** Finish a round, agree on the next fee, recruit another player, and continue without rebuilding the room.
- **Transparent game flow:** See the entry fee, round result, winnings, player readiness, and countdown clearly.
- **Telegram-native experience:** Open, play, share rooms, and invite friends directly through Telegram.
- **Three languages:** Use Carta in English, Amharic, or Afaan Oromo.
- **Mobile-friendly design:** Responsive cards, controls, and safe spacing make play comfortable on mobile screens.

## Smarter rematches

Carta keeps the action moving after every completed game:

- Every human player chooses **Agree & Play** for the next round.
- Players who leave the completed-game screen are removed without another deduction.
- A three- or four-player room can continue with the remaining ready players.
- The room creator can pause the countdown and open one seat for a new player.
- Public rooms return to the public lobby; private rooms stay private and shareable.
- The creator can propose a new next-round fee.
- A fee update resets approvals so everyone accepts the same final amount.
- New players see the rematch lobby without seeing cards from a round they did not play.
- The creator can hold or resume the response countdown when the group needs more time.
- If unanimous approval is not reached before the countdown ends, the rematch is cancelled and the creator's room returns to the lobby for a fresh start.

## Fair leaving rules

Carta clearly explains the result of leaving before any confirmation:

- Leaving an active paid round after cards are dealt keeps half of the player's entry fee as the leave penalty.
- If the opponent has not played for at least five minutes, the waiting player can leave without a deduction.
- Leaving between completed rounds is free.
- Server-side checks prevent timeout or fee rules from being bypassed by stale screens.

## A challenge that grows with you

Practice is designed to stay interesting. Carta's bot difficulty considers how many games a player has completed and how often they have won. New players receive a balanced introduction, while experienced players face stronger decisions and tougher hands. Difficulty is capped to keep the game competitive and playable.

## Birr-powered competition

- Choose a clearly displayed room entry fee.
- Deposit using the available payment instructions and receipt confirmation flow.
- Follow round pots, payouts, and personal game progress.
- Request withdrawals after meeting the displayed game and remaining-balance requirements.
- Receive eligible welcome and referral rewards directly in your Carta balance.

> Entry fees, rewards, and withdrawals are subject to Carta's in-app rules and service availability. Play responsibly and only use amounts you are comfortable with.

## How to play

1. Open Carta from the official Telegram bot.
2. Choose a public room, open a private invitation, or create your own room.
3. Confirm the room size and entry fee.
4. Draw and lay cards to build the winning **4-3-3-1** rank pattern.
5. Declare your valid winning hand.
6. Collect the displayed result and choose whether to join the next round.

## Invite your friends

Private rooms stay hidden from the public lobby and can be shared directly through Telegram. Create a room, send the invitation, and start playing when your group is ready.

Eligible referrals can also earn rewards when a new player joins through a valid Carta invitation.
